Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What's It Worth? respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use this website and how that information may be used.

Information We May Collect

When you use What's It Worth?, information may be collected automatically by the website or by services used to operate the site. This may include information such as your browser type, device type, general location information, pages visited, and technical information about your visit.

If you voluntarily contact us, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

Photos and Uploaded Content

What's It Worth? may provide tools that allow visitors to upload photographs of items for identification or valuation purposes. Uploaded photographs may contain information that you did not intend to share, so please review photographs before uploading them.

Do not upload photographs containing sensitive personal information, financial information, passwords, identification documents, or other private information.

The current photo-preview feature on this website displays selected images in your browser. Future photo-analysis features may use third-party technology to process uploaded images. Information about such processing will be updated in this policy before those features are introduced.

Cookies and Advertising

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve the website, understand visitor activity, and support advertising.

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to display advertisements and measure advertising performance.

Visitors may have options for managing advertising personalization and cookies through their browser and applicable advertising settings.

Third-Party Services

What's It Worth? may use third-party services for website hosting, analytics, advertising, security, communications, or other website functions. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to operate and improve the website, respond to communications, understand website usage, prevent abuse, maintain security, and provide services or features requested by visitors.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

This website is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the website and its features change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the contact information provided on the What's It Worth? website.
