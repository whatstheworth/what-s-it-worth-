Value Guides

A few important things to understand before putting a price on your item.

Condition Can Change Everything

Two identical items can have very different values depending on condition, completeness, damage, restoration, and packaging.

Find the Exact Model

Brand names aren't always enough. Model numbers, production markings, serial numbers, and variations can make a major difference.

Asking Prices Aren't Sold Prices

A seller can ask almost anything. Completed sales provide a much better indication of what buyers have actually paid.

Older Doesn't Always Mean Valuable

Age can matter, but rarity, demand, condition, history, and collectibility often matter just as much or more.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a photo tell me exactly what my item is worth?

A photo can provide useful clues about an item, but a photo alone cannot guarantee an exact market value. Condition, authenticity, rarity, location, and current demand can all affect value.

What should I photograph?

Take clear photos of the entire item as well as labels, logos, model numbers, markings, serial numbers, signatures, and any damage or unusual details.

How is an item's resale value determined?

Resale value can depend on condition, brand, model, rarity, authenticity, demand, location, and prices achieved by comparable items.

Is an online estimate a professional appraisal?

No. Online estimates are informational and should not be treated as a professional appraisal or guaranteed selling price.
